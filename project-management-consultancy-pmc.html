Definition of PMC

What is Project Management Consultancy (PMC)?

Project Management Consultancy is a professional service where experts oversee and coordinate the execution of your pre-planned design, construction, and interior setup on-site. Instead of you dealing with multiple vendors and contractors, our dedicated PMC team serves as your single point of contact and representative at the site, ensuring the project aligns perfectly with your timeline, approved budgets, and design aspirations. (Note: Initial architectural planning and baseline budgeting are not parts of our PMC scope, but we audit and manage execution strictly on-site.)

Sourcing, verifying credentials, contract finalization, and scheduling structural builders, carpenters, painters, and electricians.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are PMC charges? +

PMC charges are usually calculated as a percentage of the total project cost (typically ranging from 5% to 12% depending on the complexity, timeline, and physical scale of the site). For specific, well-defined scopes, we can also agree on a fixed monthly consultation fee.

Is PMC available across India? +

Yes, while our design studio is located in Sanjay Place, Agra, our project management consultancy services cover Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Mathura, Nagpur, and Gujarat. We also provide remote project management support across India for selected residential and commercial sites.

Can clients use their own contractor? +

Absolutely! As an independent consultant, we work as your representative on-site. If you already have local contractors or family labor, we will audit their work, cross-reference their bills, and ensure they meet design specs and deadlines without conflict.

Do you manage material procurement? +

Yes, we provide complete procurement guidance. We write down precise specifications, source quotes from verified dealers, and verify material quality (checking wood thickness, cement grades, and wiring standards) upon site delivery.

Do you provide weekly updates? +

Yes. We share daily status updates and photo loops via WhatsApp. Additionally, every Saturday, you will receive a comprehensive progress report (PDF) summarizing completed work, material status, expense tracking, and the upcoming week's milestone schedule.

Do you handle both architecture & interiors? +

Yes, Sagar Associates is a multidisciplinary studio. We manage civil engineering, architecture foundation work, brickwork alignment, modular kitchen fittings, lighting decoration, and finishing details under one unified PMC program.
