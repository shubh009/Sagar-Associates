What is the cost of interior design in Vrindavan? +

The cost depends heavily on your villa or apartment size, materials chosen, and scope. We offer high-quality customizable options matching standard budgets up to high-end luxury interiors. Contact us for a transparent quote.

What are the estimated interior design cost ranges in Vrindavan? +

Interior design cost packages generally scale with your requirements:
• Basic/Semi-Furnished Packages: Range between ₹150 to ₹300 per sq. ft. (covering essential woodwork, modular kitchen, and wardrobes).
• Standard Turnkey Packages (2BHK/3BHK): Typically range from ₹3.5 Lakhs to ₹7 Lakhs, including false ceiling designs, modular storage, and premium paint finish.
• Premium Luxury Interiors: Range from ₹8 Lakhs onwards (₹700 to ₹1200+ per sq. ft.), featuring custom CNC designs, profile lights, modular acrylic glass shutters, and top-tier Italian finishes.
